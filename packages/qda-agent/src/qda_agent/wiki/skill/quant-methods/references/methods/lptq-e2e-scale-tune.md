# lptq-e2e-scale-tune
Addresses: block-local and layer-local objectives leave small per-layer errors that compound along depth and cannot trade one layer's error against another's; in a fixed format, the group scales are the free parameters left after the code choice. Measured as: on a fixed text, the per-layer hidden-state distance of the quantized model to the source along depth (or its logit-lens counterpart).
Mechanism: Start from a quantized checkpoint. Freeze every integer code and zero point. Train only the group scales end to end (s = s0·exp(delta), the rounding of s to its stored dtype inside the forward, straight-through). The loss and the training data are separate choices (obj-distillation-loss; calibration.md).
Effect: the group scales move to lower the end-to-end training objective on the training text from the parent's value; codes, zero points, packing and bits stay the parent's.
Preconditions: a parent whose codes, zero points, group scales and packing re-pack without loss; a fake-quant forward that matches the serving kernel.
QDA tool: `scale_kd` is a checkpoint step. Per-module bit widths are preserved from the packed parent, including 4/8 and 3/4/8 mixes. A float build may use `format_overrides` and then this step in one recipe. Fresh overrides on an already packed source are refused: declaring another format does not repack its integer codes. The group is fixed by the search row; the agent chooses the method order.
Inference overhead: none.
Pitfalls: without the stored-dtype rounding in the training forward, the trained model cannot be saved and the objective jumps at export. Second: a group scale's gradient is a sum over the group's weights, and one group scale per group gives few parameters, so the objective can overfit the training text; that text is part of the method (calibration.md).
Origin: Chen et al. (EfficientQAT) observed that blocks interact and a local objective does not see this. So after the block-local phase they freeze the codes, which fixes the storage format, and train only the group scales under a whole-model objective.
Source: Chen et al., "EfficientQAT: Efficient Quantization-Aware Training for Large Language Models", ACL 2025 (E2E-QP). https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11062 · code: https://github.com/OpenGVLab/EfficientQAT ; Kim et al., "Memory-Efficient Fine-Tuning of Compressed Large Language Models via sub-4-bit Integer Quantization", NeurIPS 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14152
