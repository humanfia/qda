# rot-spinquant
Addresses: a random rotation flattens a given model's channel concentration by an amount that is itself a random draw. Measured as: the spread, over rotation seeds, of the rotated readers' group absmax/rms histogram and of the fake-quantised network's output error on calibration text.
Mechanism: Replace the random Hadamard of the residual rotation (rot-hadamard-r1) and of the head-wise value/output rotation (rot-hadamard-r2) with orthogonal matrices learned by Cayley SGD on the Stiefel manifold. The objective is the loss of the network with fake-quantised weights on calibration text. Then absorb both rotations into the weights as those cards do. The paper's online rotations for activation and cache quantization are not part of a weight-only build.
Effect: training lowers the fake-quantised network's objective on calibration text from its random-Hadamard initialisation; the result still depends on the initialisation and the seed; the function is unchanged for the absorbed rotations.
Preconditions: all preconditions of rot-hadamard-r1/-r2 except 2^n (a learned dense R needs no Hadamard size); memory for end-to-end training through the whole fake-quantised model; calibration text matching the deployment distribution (the rotation is fitted to it).
Inference overhead: none (both rotations are absorbed into the weights offline).
Pitfalls: ordinary gradient steps move an orthogonal parameter off the manifold, and a nearly orthogonal matrix makes the fold not an identity. This error is a small systematic transform of every residual channel, passes a loose check, and is not quantization error; assert R^T R = I in fp32 after training. Second: the learned R is fitted to the calibration text and to the exact scheme of training; reuse at a different bit width or group size drops that coupling without warning.
Origin: Liu et al. observed that the quantized network's output error varies over random rotations, although the function does not, so they optimize the rotation. Cayley optimization keeps it orthogonal, and only rotations that cancel between a writer and a reader are learned, because those fold into the weights.
Source: Liu et al., "SpinQuant: LLM quantization with learned rotations", ICLR 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16406 · code: https://github.com/facebookresearch/SpinQuant
