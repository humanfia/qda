# per-module-damage-is-context-dependent
Observable: a module's damage measured alone does not predict its damage in the joint build. Quantize one module, leave every other at source precision, and record how far the model's output moved. Do that for each module. Then quantize all of them and compare the total with the sum of the singles. The two do not agree, and not by a constant factor. The ordering of modules by single-module damage need not be the ordering of their contributions inside the full build.
How to see it on your own model: measure agreement with the source on a fixed set of positions for each single-module swap, then for the full build. This needs one source-precision checkpoint and the ability to swap one module's weights at a time. Separate the non-additivity from the repeat noise of the measurement. To see the repeat noise under the same conditions, score one unchanged module twice. A per-module difference within that noise is not a measurement of that module. The value of repairing a module also carries the model's sensitivity to that module's output (local-damage-is-not-repair-value).
